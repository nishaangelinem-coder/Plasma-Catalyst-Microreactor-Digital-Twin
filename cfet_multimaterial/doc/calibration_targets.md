# Calibration targets (Step 13)

No measured or TCAD I–V data were available for this study, so the calibration targets are
**literature-anchored synthetic reference curves** (`data/<device>_idvg_ref.csv`,
`data/<device>_idvd_ref.csv`) generated by `sim/make_reference_data.py` from the figure-of-merit
anchors below with an *independent* EKV-type device formulation (so the fit of UCM-CFET to them
is a real test of the model's functional form, not a tautology). The anchors are the authors'
reading of published ranges and roadmap targets at the platform supply voltage; the flow
accepts measured or TCAD CSV files with the same column layout as drop-in replacements.

| Device | VDD (V) | Ion (mA/µm) | Ioff (nA/µm) | SS (mV/dec) | DIBL (mV/V) | Anchor sources (refs.bib keys) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| gaa_n (Si NS n) | 0.7 | 1.00 | 1.0 | 68 | 40 | IRDS2023, Loubet2017, Mertens2016 |
| gaa_p (Si NS p) | 0.7 | 0.75 | 1.0 | 70 | 45 | IRDS2023, Loubet2017 |
| sige_p (SiGe NS p) | 0.7 | 0.95 | 1.5 | 72 | 50 | Mertens2016, Mochizuki2020 |
| mos2_n (MoS₂) | 0.5 | 0.10 | 1.0 | 72 | 55 | Li2023Nature, Shen2021, OBrien2021/2023 |
| wse2_p (WSe₂) | 0.5 | 0.07 | 1.0 | 78 | 60 | Chou2022, OBrien2023 |
| cnt_n / cnt_p (CNT array) | 0.6 | 1.50 / 1.40 | 10 | 75 | 45 | Liu2020Science, Hills2019, Deng2007 |
| gan_n (GaN 2DEG) | 1.2 | 0.50 | 1.0 | 80 | 30 | Amano2018, Chowdhury2020, Zheng2021 |
| gan_p (p-GaN, projected) | 1.2 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 85 | 30 | Bader2020, Zheng2021 |

Notes
* The 2D-material Ion anchors are deliberately below the record values (which are reported at
  |VDS| = 1 V with ≤ 50 Ω·µm contacts); at VDD = 0.5 V with 0.5–0.8 kΩ·µm contacts and the
  10⁵ Ion/Ioff constraint, 0.07–0.10 mA/µm is the self-consistent value.
* CNT anchors assume 250 tubes/µm, 99.9999 % semiconducting purity (FMET = 10⁻⁶).
* p-GaN is an *exploratory projection* (µ_h ≈ 20 cm²/Vs, Rsd ≈ 1.5 Ω·mm); it is up-sized 3× to
  partially balance the inverter.

Fitted parameters: VTH0, MU0 (TTR and ALPHA for CNT), VSAT, RSW = RDW (Si/SiGe/GaN only; the
2D and CNT contact resistance is kept at its physical nominal value because it is the subject of
the sensitivity study), LAMBDA, ETA and N0. Results: `results/calibration_mape.csv`,
`data/calibrated_params.json`, Figs. 2–4.
